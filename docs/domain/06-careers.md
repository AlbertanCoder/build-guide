## What the Job Bank data says

Job Bank groups the data engineer profile under Data scientists (NOC 21211) and
rates Alberta prospects "Moderate". The Alberta/Edmonton median is CA$43.27/hr
(Job Bank, NOC 21211, updated 19 Nov 2025).

## The classification question is separate

GoA Systems Analyst is a different classification with its own controls; Level 1
MRS accepts a related two-year diploma, or a related one-year certificate and
qualifying experience. Do not assume experience alone replaces the education
requirement — check the current MRS wording.

source:: Guide tab 5 and the MRS discussion there
verified:: 2026-11-01

## Portfolio evidence, not promises

A completed, tested project is portfolio evidence; an unbuilt guide is not
evidence of a working system, a reference, or interview fluency. No employment
outcome is guaranteed — the guide says so in its own words.

## Say only what you measured

The pitch script's rule is that you never claim a detection rate until you have
measured it. Demo kit and pitch practice live in the Career tab; the honest
version of the pitch is the one that survives the follow-up question.
