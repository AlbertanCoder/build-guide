## The forest-fire line that never bends

Do not operate over a forest-fire area or within 5 nautical miles (9.3 km) of
one below 3,000 ft AGL, and do not enter airspace restricted by a NOTAM under
CAR 601.16. Unauthorised drones ground air tankers and endanger crews.

## Register, mark, and carry the certificate

Register and mark the drone (250 g to 25 kg) and carry proof plus a Pilot
Certificate — Basic Operations. The Small Basic exam and registration have fees
(exam CA$10.17 plus registration CA$10.17, per TC's fee schedule, checked
2026-11-03); age 14+ applies to Basic. The certificate does not expire, but you
may fly only if, within the last 24 months, you got the certificate, passed a
Transport Canada drone exam again, passed a flight review, or completed
recurrent training; carry the certificate and proof of that whenever you fly
(CAR 901.56(1), 901.57).

source:: Guide: Buy, Stage 7 (fees checked 2026-11-03); Transport Canada fee schedule, via the order list; recency: CAR 901.56(1) and 901.57 (Guide, Fly safely, Papers first)
verified:: 2026-11-10

## Altitude, proximity and weight

Fly below 400 ft (122 m) AGL, and no more than 30 m above a building while
within 61 m of it horizontally (CAR 901.25). Operating weight must be no more
than 25 kg — measure the completed aircraft rather than trusting estimates.
Maintain VLOS.

source:: Guide, Fly safely (Where & when you may fly)
verified:: 2026-11-03

## Night lights are necessary, not sufficient

CAR 901.39 requires the aircraft to remain visible; a generic LED kit does not
by itself make a flight legal. VLOS, airspace, bystander, aerodrome, NOTAM,
Alberta fire bans and local rules all still apply at night.

source:: Guide, Fly safely, Step 6 (night-lighting note)
verified:: 2026-11-03
